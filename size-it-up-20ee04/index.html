How to play

Five rounds. One hundred points each.

  1. A reference object sits on the left, drawn at its true size. You know how big it is, and the bracket marks the dimension its size refers to.
  2. Drag the corner handle on the crimson silhouette until it looks like the right size next to that reference. The label tells you which dimension counts.
  3. Hit Lock In. You score on how close your ratio was — guessing half the true size costs exactly as much as guessing double.

The two are always measured on the same axis — a length against a length, a height against a height — so you are never asked to judge one against the other.

Use + and − to zoom when something runs off the canvas, and drag the background to pan. Arrow keys nudge the handle once it has focus.
